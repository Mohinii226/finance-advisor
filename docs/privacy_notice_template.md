# Privacy notice for FinSight (version 2026-10-v1)

**Who we are.** [[Your full name or organisation]] ("we") runs FinSight, a personal-finance analysis service. We decide why and how
your personal data is processed. Contact for any privacy question or complaint (grievance officer): [[name]], [[email]], [[postal address]].
We aim to reply within [[7]] days.

## What we collect and why
| Data | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Name, email address | Create your account, verify your email, send password-reset links |
| Password | Log you in. We store only a salted one-way hash, never the password |
| Monthly income (optional) | Recognise your salary credits and compute your savings plan |
| Transactions from the statements you upload: date, short description (long account / reference numbers are masked), amount, balance | Categorise your spending and show your dashboard, alerts, forecast, health score and answers in the chat |
| Technical data: login times, session id (stored hashed), rate-limit counters (hashed), server logs | Keep the service secure and stop abuse |

We **do not** store the uploaded file itself, sell your data, or use it for advertising.
You must be **18 or older**. We do not knowingly process children's data.

## Who else sees it
- [[Hosting provider, country]] hosts our servers.
- [[Email provider]] delivers verification and reset emails (it sees your email address and the message).
- **Chat assistant.** [[Choose one and delete the other:]]
  (a) The chat uses software running only on our own servers. Your data is not sent to any AI provider.
  (b) Your chat questions and the spending figures needed to answer them are sent to Anthropic (USA) to generate the answer.

## Your consent and your rights
You give consent when you create an account and again before you upload a statement. You can **withdraw consent** at any time under
*Your data* in the app; withdrawing statement consent stops further processing. You can also:
- **delete your imported transactions** or **delete your whole account** under *Your data* (immediate; this erases your login, profile and transactions);
- ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold, to correct it, or to complain: write to the contact above;
- nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
If we cannot resolve your complaint you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## How long we keep it
Until you delete it or your account. Database backups are kept for [[14]] days, so deleted data disappears from backups within that
time. Server logs are kept for [[90]] days. [[Add any longer retention you are legally required to keep, with the reason.]]

## How we protect it
Encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, per-user access control (you can only ever see your own data), login rate limiting,
email verification, a private database that is not reachable from the internet, and regular backups. If a personal-data breach
affects you we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
